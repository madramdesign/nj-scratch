Privacy Policy

NJ Scratch-Off Tracker website and the NJ Scratch app for iPhone and iPad. Effective November 25, 2026.

Summary

We don’t collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Watchlist (games you star) and display settings are saved only on your device: in the app’s local storage on iPhone and iPad, or in your browser’s local storage on the website. They are never sent to us.
  • Cached data: the app keeps a copy of the latest public prize data so it works offline. You can remove it by deleting the app.

Network requests

The app and website download public data files from this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log basic request information such as IP addresses; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Links to njlottery.com open the New Jersey Lottery’s own website, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is intended for adults. You must be 18 or older to buy New Jersey Lottery tickets.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
