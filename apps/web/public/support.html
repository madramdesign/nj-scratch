Support

NJ Scratch-Off Tracker website and the NJ Scratch app for iPhone and iPad.

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/madramdesign/nj-scratch/issues. Please include the game number and what you expected to see.

Common questions

Where does the data come from? The official New Jersey Lottery instant-games data published on njlottery.com, checked about every 6 hours. Values marked “est.” are this app’s calculations, not official figures.

Does a high score mean I’ll win? No. Scores compare the remaining official prizes of active games. Every game returns less than it costs on average.

The data looks out of date. The app shows when the data was last verified. Pull down to refresh. If it stays stale for more than a day, please open an issue.

How do I remove my data? Nothing leaves your device. Deleting the app removes your watchlist and cached data. See the privacy policy.

Responsible play

Scratch-off tickets are gambling. You must be 18 or older to buy New Jersey Lottery tickets. If gambling is a problem, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
